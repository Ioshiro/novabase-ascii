## Capabilities and Constraints

- NovaNet: SCADA developed in-house on .NET; talks to any operator panel or PLC; centralised view; usable from phone or tablet.
- BTW: battery-powered wireless probes developed in-house, for real-time monitoring of environmental variables, also in hostile environments.
- Custom electronics and software: from management software to on-machine operator panels, with after-sales support.
- On-site probe calibration with a portable oil bath and primary thermometer; certificate issued.
- Plant mapping with BTW probes inside ovens and cells, empty or loaded, finding hot and cold spots.
